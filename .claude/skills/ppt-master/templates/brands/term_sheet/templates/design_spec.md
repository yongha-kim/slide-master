# Term Sheet Brand Specification

> Identity-only preset. No SVG page roster — pages are composed freely under these constraints.
>
> **Provenance**: distilled from the user-supplied `Term Sheet_PROMPT.md` theme prompt (a structured, institutional investor pitch deck in the spirit of the Sequoia pitch template — calm authority over flash). Page-level look (numbered section grid, ruled tables, bordered panels, action titles) lives in the paired visual style `consulting-action`, not here.
>
> Install-local Pretendard lock applies: the source's Source Serif 4 / IBM Plex Sans pairing is translated into Pretendard weight and size contrast — no serif or Plex substitution.

## I. Brand Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Brand Name | Term Sheet |
| Use Cases | 투자 유치 피치, IR 자료, 투자자 미팅, 펀딩 라운드, 기관투자자 대상 사업 소개 |
| Tone | Calm institutional authority — structured, evidence-first, never flashy |
| Recommended pairing | mode: `pyramid` (story-led pitch: `narrative`) · visual_style: `consulting-action` · delivery_purpose: `balanced` · image_usage: `none` · icons: `none` |

**Anti-mood**: "startup hype deck", "gradient hero", "stock-photo optimism", "icon grid", "rounded blob illustration".

## II. Color Scheme

| Role | HEX | Provenance | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| bg | `#F7F8FA` | user | Flat cool white on every slide — never a gradient |
| surface | `#FFFFFF` | user | Panel / table surface on the cool-white field |
| border | `#DDE2EA` | user | 1px panel borders, table rules, hairlines |
| headline | `#0E1B33` | user | Headlines |
| primary (navy) | `#16315C` | user | Section numbers, table rules and headers, bar fills, stat numbers |
| primary-soft | `#DCE4F2` | user | One highlight panel per page at most |
| text | `#41495A` | user | Body text |
| label | `#818A9C` | user | Uppercase section labels, captions, footers |

Usage conventions that travel with the identity: navy `#16315C` is the only accent hue — it carries headings, section numbers, table rules, and bar fills and nothing else; exactly one panel per page may sit on `#DCE4F2`; every other panel is `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#DDE2EA` border; no second accent hue, no decorative gradients, no drop shadows.

## III. Typography

| Role | Family | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| headline | `'Pretendard SemiBold', Pretendard, 'Malgun Gothic', sans-serif` | 600 |
| stat number | `'Pretendard SemiBold', Pretendard, 'Malgun Gothic', sans-serif` | 600 |
| body | `Pretendard, 'Malgun Gothic', sans-serif` | 400 |
| section label | `'Pretendard Medium', Pretendard, 'Malgun Gothic', sans-serif` | 500 |

> Install-local Pretendard lock (`references/strategist.md` §g). Source proportions to keep as ratios against the locked body size: headlines ≈ 2.4–3.6× body, stat numbers ≈ 2.4–3× body, labels ≈ 0.7× body in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (Latin labels only). The serif headline character is carried by the 600 weight plus a large size step, not by a second family.

## IV. Logo

- None. No logo is bundled. Text-only self-identification is permitted (company name on the cover and in the footer).

## V. Voice & Tone

- Formality: formal-institutional
- Person: we (the company) addressing investors; no direct-address hype
- Emoji: forbidden
- Abbreviations: spell-out-first-use (ARR, CAC, LTV etc. defined once)
- Claims discipline: every metric states its unit, period, and basis; forward-looking numbers are labeled as projections
- Forbidden: superlatives without evidence, centered body paragraphs, photos or icons as proof

## VI. Icon Style

- Preference: none

> Icons are excluded from this identity. Taxonomy is carried by section numbers, uppercase labels, rules, and tables.
